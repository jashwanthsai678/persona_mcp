To connect it in Claude:

  1. Open Claude (claude.ai or Claude Desktop) and go to Settings → Connectors
  2. Choose Add custom connector (sometimes labeled "Add more")
  3. Paste the URL above as the connector's remote server URL
  4. Save, then enable it for your conversation
  5. Try asking Claude something like "ask Radhima how the Goa trip planning is going"
